Journal Article · Biomolecules · 2024

De Novo Design of Inhibitors of DNA Methyltransferase 1: A Critical Comparison of Ligand- and Structure-Based Approaches

Citation: Diana L. Prado-Romero, Fernanda I. Saldívar-González, Iván López-Mata, Pedro A. Laurel-García, Adrián Durán-Vargas, Enrique García-Hernández, Norberto Sánchez-Cruz, and José L. Medina-Franco. Biomolecules 14(7), 775.

The study compares ligand-based and structure-based de novo molecular design strategies for constructing screening libraries focused on DNA methyltransferase 1 (DNMT1), an epigenetic target relevant to drug discovery.

This work was developed during my research-volunteer training with Dr. Norberto Sánchez-Cruz at the Chemistry Institute of the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), from September 2023 to May 2024.

Technical Book

Setting Up Computational Material Science Clusters

Author: Ivan Lopez Mata.

This technical book presents a practical introduction to preparing computing infrastructure for computational materials science, connecting hardware, Linux-based environments, and the execution of scientific workloads.

The idea grew from my technical training and my work as a laboratory technician in Dr. Jose Miguel Mora Fonz's modelling laboratory at Universidad Juarez Autonoma de Tabasco (UJAT), where I worked from February 2023 to June 2025.

Conference Paper · Springer · 2026

Detection of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus in Individuals with Normal Fasting Glucose Using a Multilayer Perceptron Neural Network

Citation: Rubén Alfredo Peraza-Torres, Iván López-Mata, Alejandro Medina-Santiago, Ivonne Adriana Hernández-Hernández, José Antonio Cortes-García, and Jorge Antonio Orozco-Torres. In Emerging Trends in Information Systems and Technologies, WorldCIST 2025, Lecture Notes in Networks and Systems, vol. 1514, pp. 625–644.

The study applies a multilayer perceptron neural network to clinical records from a hospital information system to support the early detection of type 2 diabetes, including individuals whose fasting glucose values remain within the normal range.

This publication resulted from my Data Science Internship at SIA Software, from October 2024 to January 2025, as part of a precision-medicine platform being developed using clinical information provided by local hospitals.
