Hard Matter: Experimental and Computational Projects

These two complementary group reports were completed as part of the evaluation for Hard Matter: Fundamentals and Applications. The first focused on the experimental characterization of solid-state materials, while the second used first-principles calculations to study the electronic and magnetic properties of FeS2 marcasite.

Reports

Experimental Report

Characterization of AgI and Gd-doped CeO2 through DSC, XRPD, UV-Vis diffuse reflectance, EIS, SEM/EMPA, TEM and Raman spectroscopy.

View PDF Download

Computational Report

DFT study of the electronic and magnetic structure of orthorhombic FeS2 marcasite using Quantum ESPRESSO and VASP.
