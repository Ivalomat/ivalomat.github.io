Get to Know Me

“Find solutions, not problems.”

My elementary school teacher repeated this phrase so often that it eventually became part of the way I think. I learned early not to wait for ideal circumstances before taking action.

Growing up in a small town in Mexico, I became accustomed to finding practical ways forward. When I first needed computing power for a scientific project, I assembled a working server from broken and discarded computers available at my university. Even travelling to academic competitions in the back of a pickup truck reinforced the same lesson: the conditions in which you begin do not have to limit the seriousness of your ambitions.

These experiences shaped the way I approach challenges today. I work comfortably within constraints, learn what I need to learn, and turn available resources into functional solutions. For me, resourcefulness is not simply improvising-it is understanding what a problem requires and creating a path forward.
